Privacy Policy

Hold Paws: Otter Escape Puzzle · Last revised: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Hold Paws mobile game ("the game", "we", "us") uses, why, who receives it and what choices you have. Please read it together with our Terms of Service. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the game and delete it from your device.

1. Who we are

Hold Paws is made by Hüseyin Aydıngün, an independent developer in Türkiye. He is the data controller for the information described here (the "veri sorumlusu" under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law, KVKK, and the "controller" under the EU and UK GDPR). Contact: holdpawsgame@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • You do not need an account. We never ask for your real name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, photos, location or payment details.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • Leaderboards use an anonymous player ID and the profile name you choose.
  • Ads are shown by Unity LevelPlay and its ad networks. On iOS, the advertising identifier is used for tracking only if you allow it.
  • Payments are handled only by Apple or Google.

3. Information kept on your device

The game saves on your device: your progress (level, score), helper uses, purchased items, your profile (the name you type, avatar and frame) and your settings (language, music, sound, character voices, vibration, ad choice). We do not receive this information. You can erase it with "Reset game" in the settings or by deleting the game.

4. Leaderboards (Unity Gaming Services)

The game has regional and global leaderboards run by Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies). When you open the home screen or finish a level, the game signs in without an account: Unity gives your device an anonymous player ID. The game then sends your score, the profile name, avatar and frame, and the country board chosen from your device's region setting. Unity also receives technical data needed to answer the request, such as your IP address and device type. Other players can see your profile name, avatar, frame and score. Choose a name that does not identify you. Names pass through a filter, and we may remove names that are offensive.

5. Advertising

The game is free and is funded by ads: optional rewarded ads (for example to get hearts or helpers) and ads between levels. Ads are delivered by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and the ad networks it works with, currently Unity Ads and ironSource Ads. To choose, show, limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, these partners can collect from your device:

  • device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) and the vendor identifier (IDFV);
  • IP address and an approximate location derived from it (country or city level, never precise GPS location);
  • device and network information: model, operating system, language, time zone, carrier, connection type;
  • ad interaction data: which ads were shown, clicked, watched or skipped, and app events such as sessions.

Your choices. On iOS, the game asks for your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before the advertising identifier can be used for tracking. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not based on your activity in other apps. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you are in the EU, the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the game also asks whether ads may be personalised; you can change your answer in the game settings (Ad choices). On Android you can delete or reset your Advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads. Buying "No Ads" removes the ads between levels; rewarded ads appear only when you choose to watch them.

If you tap an ad, you may leave the game for a website or store page. Those sites and the advertisers have their own privacy policies.

6. Purchases

Purchases (Starter Pack, No Ads, helper packs) are made through the Apple App Store or Google Play, which handle payment under their own terms. We never receive your card or payment details. The game receives a confirmation (receipt) of what you bought so it can give you the item and restore it later.

7. Support and feedback e-mails

If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and message. We use them only to answer you and improve the game, and we delete them when they are no longer needed (normally within 12 months).

8. Why we use information (legal bases)

  • To run the game, leaderboards and purchases you ask for (performance of our agreement with you).
  • To show the ads that fund the free game, to limit how often ads appear and to prevent fraud (our legitimate interest); personalised ads and tracking only with your consent where the law requires it.
  • To answer your messages (legitimate interest, or your request).
  • To meet legal duties, such as sales and tax records (legal obligation).

We do not make decisions about you that have legal or similar effects based only on automated processing.

9. Who receives information

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services, LevelPlay, Unity Ads) and ironSource, as service providers and, for ads, as independent partners: Unity privacy policy, ironSource privacy policy.
  • Apple and Google, for downloads and purchases, under their own policies.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of players or us.
  • A buyer or successor, if the game is ever sold or transferred; this policy would continue to apply.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Under some US state laws (for example California's CCPA/CPRA), letting ad partners use device identifiers for personalised ads can count as "sharing" or "selling". You can stop this by turning off tracking (section 5) or by writing to us.

10. International transfers

Our partners may process information on servers outside your country, including in the United States and the EU. They use legal safeguards for such transfers, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. For transfers from Türkiye, the safeguards of KVKK article 9 apply.

11. How long information is kept

Data on your device stays until you reset or delete the game. Leaderboard entries stay while the leaderboard runs; we may reset boards at any time. Ad partners keep data for the periods in their own policies. E-mails: see section 7.

12. Children

Hold Paws is a general-audience puzzle game; it is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country, such as 16 in some EU countries). Children should use the game only with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under KVKK, the GDPR or US state laws), you can ask us whether we process your information, for a copy of it, to correct it, to delete it (including your leaderboard entries), to restrict or object to its use, or withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier use. Write to holdpawsgame@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. Because we do not know who you are, please send your profile name and, if you can, the date you last played, so we can find your entries. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye: Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu). Information that ad partners hold can also be managed through their own tools and policies.

14. Security

We work with partners that protect data with industry-standard measures, and we keep the information we receive to a minimum. No method of sending or storing data is completely safe, so we cannot promise absolute security.

15. Changes

We may update this policy. We change the date at the top, and for important changes we also give notice in the game or on this site. Using the game after a change means you accept the new policy.

16. Contact

Questions or requests: holdpawsgame@gmail.com.
